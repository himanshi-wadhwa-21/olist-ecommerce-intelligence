# Business Requirements

## Project Overview

The Olist E-commerce Customer & Operations Intelligence project analyses customer reviews, delivery performance, product revenue and customer complaint patterns.

The purpose is to turn raw e-commerce data into useful business insights that can help teams understand customer experience and identify areas for operational improvement.

## Business Problem

An e-commerce business needs to understand more than how many orders it receives or how much revenue it generates.

Customer feedback and delivery performance can reveal problems that may not be visible through sales figures alone.

The project explores questions such as:

- How are customers rating their shopping experience?
- What is the overall sentiment expressed in written reviews?
- Are late deliveries associated with more negative customer feedback?
- Which complaint themes appear most frequently?
- How does customer experience change over time?
- Which areas should the business investigate first?

## Project Objectives

1. Analyse customer reviews and ratings to understand customer experience.
2. Use sentiment analysis to classify written customer feedback.
3. Compare customer feedback across delivery performance groups.
4. Identify recurring complaint themes.
5. Analyse product revenue and other e-commerce performance indicators.
6. Present the findings through an interactive Power BI report.

## Scope of Analysis

The project includes:

- Exploratory analysis of Olist e-commerce data.
- SQL-based data querying and analysis.
- Python-based data preparation and analysis.
- Sentiment classification of written customer reviews.
- Exploratory keyword-based complaint categorisation.
- Power BI dashboards for customer experience, AI insights, delivery intelligence, complaint analysis and executive reporting.

## Expected Business Value

The analysis is intended to help business teams:

- Identify delivery-related customer experience problems.
- Understand the main sources of customer dissatisfaction.
- Monitor customer sentiment and ratings.
- Prioritise recurring complaint areas for further investigation.
- Support operational decisions using data rather than assumptions.

## Intended Users

The report may be useful to:

- Business analysts
- E-commerce operations teams
- Customer experience teams
- Customer support managers
- Business and management stakeholders

## Limitations

The analysis is based on the available dataset and does not represent every possible customer interaction.

Sentiment classification and complaint categorisation are automated analytical methods and may contain classification errors. Complaint themes are exploratory and may overlap.

The findings indicate patterns and relationships in the data. They should not be treated as proof of cause and effect.
